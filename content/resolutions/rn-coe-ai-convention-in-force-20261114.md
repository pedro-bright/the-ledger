## Decision

The challenge is sustained on both allegations.
The asserted entry into force on November 1, 2025 is a timeline error, and the asserted ratifications by the United Kingdom, France and Norway are an unsupported inference.
The event keeps its ID and date so existing links still resolve, and the date identifies the original assertion only.
The summary is corrected through [a summary revision](https://github.com/pedro-bright/the-ledger/blob/main/content/revisions/sr-coe-ai-convention-in-force-20261114.md).
The original narrative stays in the event as published, below the correction note.

## Evidence

Each finding rests on the depositary record, read from an archived copy because the live page refuses automated requests.

- [Council of Europe Treaty Office, chart of signatures and ratifications of Treaty 225](https://www.coe.int/en/web/conventions/full-list?module=signatures-by-treaty&treatynum=225), status as of November 7, 2026.
  [Archived copy, captured 2026-11-07](https://web.archive.org/web/20261107121727/https://www.coe.int/en/web/conventions/full-list?module=signatures-by-treaty&treatynum=225).
  The chart states the entry-into-force condition as "5 Ratifications including at least 3 member States of the Council of Europe" and totals "2" ratifications and "19" signatures not followed by ratification.

The September 12, 2026 reading cited in the challenge also found no entry-into-force date.
The November 7 chart confirms it and adds the two ratifications recorded since.
