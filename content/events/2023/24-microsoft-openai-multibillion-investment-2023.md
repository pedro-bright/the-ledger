## Summary

On January 23, 2023, Microsoft announced "the third phase" of its partnership with OpenAI, "a multiyear, multibillion dollar investment," following its investments in 2019 and 2021.
Neither company disclosed the amount; CNBC noted earlier reporting by Semafor that Microsoft had been in talks to invest as much as $10 billion.
Azure remained OpenAI's exclusive cloud provider, and the announcement came about two months after ChatGPT's release in late November 2022.

## What Happened

Microsoft announced the investment in a post on its official blog, and OpenAI announced it the same day on its own blog.
Microsoft said the agreement extended the two companies' collaboration on AI supercomputing and research and "enables each of us to independently commercialize the resulting advanced AI technologies."
It said it would increase its investment in specialized supercomputing systems for OpenAI's research, deploy OpenAI's models across its consumer and enterprise products, including the Azure OpenAI Service, and that "as OpenAI's exclusive cloud provider, Azure will power all OpenAI workloads across research, products and API services."

Microsoft chief executive Satya Nadella said the next phase would give developers and organizations "access to the best AI infrastructure, models, and toolchain with Azure."
OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman said that "Microsoft shares our values and we are excited to continue our independent research."
OpenAI's post said the company "remains a capped-profit company and is governed by the OpenAI non-profit."

CNBC reported that Microsoft declined to give a dollar amount and that Semafor had reported earlier in January that Microsoft was in talks to invest as much as $10 billion.
CNBC also recalled that Microsoft's $1 billion investment in July 2019 had made it OpenAI's exclusive cloud provider.

## Why It Matters

The announcement tied the most visible AI product of the moment, ChatGPT, to one of the largest technology companies through an undisclosed investment reported in the billions of dollars.
It set the pattern of a cloud provider investing in an AI lab while supplying its computing, which Google and Amazon followed with Anthropic later in 2023 ([Google investment in Anthropic](/events/anthropic-google-investment)).

The entry is the starting point of the AI valuation cycle thread.
The arrangements announced here, including Azure exclusivity and OpenAI's capped-profit structure under nonprofit governance, were renegotiated in October 2025 and again in April 2026 ([2025 renegotiation](/events/microsoft-openai-partnership-renegotiation); [2026 restructuring](/events/openai-microsoft-partnership-restructuring)).
