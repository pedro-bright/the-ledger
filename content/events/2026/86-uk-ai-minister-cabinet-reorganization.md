## Summary

On July 20, 2026, the UK government appointed Kanishka Narayan as Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence, a joint role in the Cabinet Office and the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade, and granted him attendance at Cabinet meetings.
The appointment elevated an existing junior AI portfolio to the government's senior decision-making table while Prime Minister Andy Burnham abolished the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology and redistributed its functions.

## What Happened

The government's ministerial appointments notice named Narayan as Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence and stated that he would attend Cabinet.
The role is jointly based in the Cabinet Office and the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade.
Narayan had served from September 2025 to July 2026 as a Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State in the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology, where his portfolio included artificial intelligence and online safety.

Bloomberg reported that Burnham created the Cabinet-attending role as part of a wider government reorganization that abolished the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology.
Most science, innovation, and business functions moved into the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade, while the joint Cabinet Office appointment placed the AI portfolio partly within the center of government.

The change did not make Narayan a Secretary of State or a full member of Cabinet.
It made him a Minister of State with permission to attend Cabinet meetings, a distinction that gives the AI portfolio access to senior deliberations without creating a standalone department or cabinet secretary for artificial intelligence.

Narayan's promotion followed his public call at the June G7 summit for greater British sovereign AI capability after US export restrictions disrupted access to Anthropic models abroad.
The July appointment did not itself announce a new compute program, regulatory framework, or procurement policy, and the government had not yet published detailed responsibilities for the role.

## Why It Matters

The reorganization changed the institutional location of British AI policy.
Instead of remaining one responsibility inside a standalone technology department, AI became a cross-government ministerial portfolio spanning the Cabinet Office and a department combining business, science, innovation, and trade.
That structure connects model policy, public-sector adoption, industrial strategy, and international competitiveness more directly to central government coordination.

Cabinet attendance can increase the portfolio's influence over decisions that cut across departments, including infrastructure, public procurement, national security, workforce policy, and regulation.
The abolition of the department previously responsible for AI also creates uncertainty about administrative ownership, staffing, and accountability while functions are redistributed.

Whether the change becomes a durable model of AI governance depends on the authority, budget, and responsibilities assigned to the new office.
At the time of appointment, the government had established senior political access but had not yet shown how that access would translate into policy or implementation.
